What we collect

  • Contact information you type in: name, email, mobile phone, ZIP code, city or mailing address.
  • Your project: room measurements, the floors you pick, extras, and room photos you choose to send us.
  • How you use the app: the floor types, products and colors you look at, your searches, samples you add, and floors you try in your room photo. We use our own app to record this. We do not use third-party ad trackers or ad networks.

How we use it

  • To give you a quote, mail samples, and contact you about your project.
  • To improve the app and our product selection.
  • Promotions, only if you say yes: if you check the email or text box, we send you deals, new floors and tips that fit what you looked at. You can say no and still get a quote. Every email has an unsubscribe link. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only with the people who do your job (our installers and suppliers), service providers that help us run the app and send messages (bound by contract to use it only for us), or when the law requires it.

Keeping it

We keep your information until you ask us to delete it, or 3 years after your last activity, whichever comes first. Records we must keep for taxes or contracts are kept as the law requires.

Your choices and California privacy rights

If you live in California (CCPA/CPRA), you can ask us to: know what personal information we have about you and how we use it, get a copy, correct it, delete it, and opt out of any sale or sharing (we do neither). We will not treat you differently for using these rights. To make a request, call (818) 203-1168 or email gomezflooringcompany@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request". We will confirm your identity using the email or phone you gave us, and answer within 45 days. You can also tap "Delete my info" in the app to erase what is saved on your phone.

Photos and camera

The app uses your camera only when you choose to take a room photo or scan a room. Photos stay on your phone unless you send them with a quote request.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date above and show the new policy in the app.
