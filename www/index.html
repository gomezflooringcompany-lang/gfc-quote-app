Measure your rooms

Enter each room wall to wall. Not sure how? Open the tape measure guide below.

How to measure with a tape measure (2 minutes)
  1. Grab a tape measureA 25 ft tape is best. A helper makes it easier, but you can do it alone.
  2. Measure the long wallHook the tape on one wall at floor level, pull it straight to the opposite wall, and read the number. Example: 14' 7" means 14 feet 7 inches. Always round up to the next inch.
  3. Measure the short wallDo the same the other way across the room.
  4. Odd shape? Split it into boxes Measure box A as the room, then add box B (or a closet) as the extra area.
  5. Count doorways and stairsCount the doorways where the new floor meets a different floor. Count your staircases.
  6. Take 3 photosOne from each corner of the room, and one of the doorway. They help us confirm your price without a drive out.
Walk slowly around the room and point the camera at every wall. The app measures the floor for you.

Camera measuring works in the Gomez Flooring app on iPhone Pro models. On other phones, use the tape measure guide.
