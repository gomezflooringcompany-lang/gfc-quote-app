## App privacy answers (Apple "App Privacy" + Google "Data safety")
- **Tracking (Apple ATT):** No. We do not track users across other companies' apps or websites, so no tracking prompt is needed. Do NOT add Facebook/Google ad SDKs without revisiting this.
- **Data linked to the user:** Contact info (name, email, phone, ZIP/address); User content (room photos, only if sent); Usage data / product interaction (floors, colors, searches viewed in the app).
- **Purposes:** App functionality, Product personalization, Developer's advertising or marketing (first-party promos, only with opt-in), Analytics.
- **Data sold / shared with third parties:** No.
- **Deletion:** in-app "Delete my info" link, plus email/phone requests.
- Email/text promos require the unchecked opt-in boxes in the app (CAN-SPAM unsubscribe in every email; TCPA: texts only with the separate text box, STOP to opt out).
